Acceptable Use Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("Policy") governs all use of MailMover. It applies in addition to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. By using MailMover, you agree to comply with this Policy at all times.

1. Permitted Use

MailMover is a tool for individuals and businesses to move their own email between mailboxes. Permitted use includes:

  • Migrating mail when changing email provider or domain
  • Consolidating or backing up mailboxes you are authorised to access
  • Testing and evaluating the service on the free tier, where offered

2. Prohibited Use

You must not use MailMover to:

2.1 Illegal or harmful activity

  • Facilitate any activity that violates applicable local, national, or international law
  • Conduct fraud, identity theft, phishing, or social engineering
  • Access, copy or move mail from a mailbox without the owner's authorisation
  • Circumvent, probe, or attack third-party systems, accounts, or networks

2.2 Platform abuse

  • Use automated scripts to overload the service or bypass concurrency or rate limits
  • Attempt to bypass authentication, access controls or other security measures
  • Create multiple accounts or sessions to circumvent plan restrictions or free-tier limits
  • Resell or sublicense access to the service without written permission

2.3 Harmful content

  • Transfer, store or distribute content that is sexually exploitative of children, or that is hateful, violent, or used to harass, stalk, or threaten individuals
  • Use the service to send spam or to relay unsolicited bulk messages

2.4 Data and privacy violations

  • Process personal data of others through the service without appropriate legal authority
  • Use leaked or stolen credentials, or private communications obtained unlawfully

3. Child Safety — Absolute Prohibition

MailMover enforces a zero-tolerance policy regarding any content or activity related to the sexual exploitation, abuse, or endangerment of minors. Any such use will result in:

  • Immediate blocking and termination of access
  • Reporting to law enforcement and relevant authorities
  • A permanent ban

4. Enforcement

Violations of this Policy may result in:

  • Stopping of running transfers and suspension of access
  • Permanent termination of access without refund
  • Reporting to legal authorities where required by law

Enforcement decisions are made at Heaventree Ltd.'s discretion to protect users, the platform, and legal compliance.

5. Reporting Violations

If you believe MailMover is being used in violation of this Policy, report it to legal@mailmover.eu. Include a description of the concern and your contact information for follow-up.

6. Policy Updates

This Policy may be updated to reflect changes in legal requirements, platform features, or emerging risks. Continued use of MailMover constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy. Material changes will be communicated by email to registered or paying users.
