How to create a Gmail app password

An app password is a 16-character code that lets one service into your mailbox. Google requires it here, and you can delete it at any time.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification. Sign in to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/security and turn on 2-Step Verification if it is not on yet.
  2. Create the app password. Open myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, type MailMover as the name and choose Create.
  3. Copy the code. Copy the 16-character code. Google shows it only once.
  4. Use it in MailMover. Choose Gmail / Workspace (server imap.gmail.com), enter the full email address and paste the code as the password. You can paste it with or without spaces.

If the app password page is missing

  • On a Google Workspace (work or school) address, your administrator may not have allowed app passwords. Ask them to turn on 2-Step Verification for your account and to allow app passwords.
  • IMAP must also be on. In Gmail, open Settings, then Forwarding and POP/IMAP, and make sure IMAP is enabled.

When you are done

After the transfer you can delete the app password from the same Google page, which cuts off its access straight away.
