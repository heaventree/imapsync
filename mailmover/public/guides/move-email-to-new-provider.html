How to move email to a new provider

Moving mail copies every folder and message from your old mailbox into the new one. This takes six steps and you can check each before anything changes.

  1. Get sign-in details for both mailboxes. Find the server name and the password you will use for each mailbox. Gmail, Outlook.com, Yahoo and iCloud need an app password instead of your normal password.
  2. Open MailMover and pick your providers. Choose the provider for the old mailbox and for the new one, or Custom IMAP. Enter the full email address and password for each.
  3. Test both logins. Choose Test Both Logins. It signs in to both mailboxes without moving anything. If one fails, MailMover says which.
  4. Do a dry run. Run a dry run first. It lists what would be copied, and changes nothing in either mailbox.
  5. Start the transfer. Choose Transfer to copy the mail and keep the original, or Move to remove it from the old mailbox after it is copied. Watch the live progress: messages copied, messages left and time left.
  6. Check the new mailbox. When it finishes, open the new mailbox and look through the folders. Keep the old mailbox until you are happy that everything is there.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the mailbox and on both providers. In one of our tests, a mailbox of about 4,400 messages (around 310 MB) took roughly 20 minutes. Yours may be faster or slower, and MailMover shows the time left as it goes.

What is kept

Folders, read and unread flags, and message dates are kept. Your passwords are used only for the transfer and are not stored.
