Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026 · Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MailMover ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the MailMover service at mailmover.eu. It applies to all visitors, users and subscribers.

1. Data Controller

Platform: MailMover
Legal Entity: Heaventree Ltd.
Jurisdiction of Registration: Gibraltar
Registered Address: Suite 4.3.02, Block 4, Eurotowers, Gibraltar, GX11 1AA
Contact (Privacy & Data Requests): legal@mailmover.eu

Heaventree Ltd. is the data controller for the purposes of applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") where applicable. Heaventree Ltd. is a company duly incorporated and registered under the laws of Gibraltar.

2. Personal Data We Process

a) Information you provide

  • Name and email address, where you buy a plan or contact us
  • Billing information (handled entirely by Stripe; we do not see card numbers)
  • Communications you send to support

Legal basis: contract performance, legitimate interests.

b) Transfer details you enter, processed only to run your transfer

  • Mail server names and ports, and mailbox usernames, for the source and destination
  • Mailbox passwords or access tokens (used for the run only and never retained; see section 9)
  • Mailbox content, which passes between your two providers in transit and is not stored by us

Legal basis: contract performance (carrying out the transfer you request).

c) Automatically collected information

  • IP address and browser type in server and network logs, for security, fraud and abuse prevention
  • Transfer status information: folder names, message counts and sizes, and technical status lines shown in your run log

Legal basis: legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service operation).

d) What we do not collect or keep

  • The content of your email, which we never store, read or analyse
  • Your mailbox passwords, which are deleted when a run ends
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-site tracking data

3. Cookies and Browser Storage

MailMover sets no cookies. We use no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

Your browser's local storage is used only for conveniences on your own device: your chosen layout, saved presets (server names, usernames and options, never passwords), and the reference to your most recent transfer so the page can reconnect after a refresh. This data stays in your browser and is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

4. How We Use Personal Data

We use personal data to:

  • Provide and operate MailMover and carry out the transfers you request
  • Manage plans and subscription status
  • Process payments through Stripe and prevent fraud
  • Communicate with you about your account and service status
  • Monitor platform security, detect abuse, and prevent unauthorised access
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell personal data. We do not use personal data or mailbox content to train AI models.

5. AI and Model Training

MailMover does not use your mailbox content, credentials, transfer activity, usage patterns, or any personal data to train, fine-tune, evaluate, or improve any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, whether operated by us or by any third party.

6. Payments

All payments are processed by Stripe. MailMover does not store, process, or have access to full payment card details or banking information. Stripe acts as an independent data processor under its own privacy policy.

We receive from Stripe only: your Stripe customer ID, subscription status, and plan.

We share personal data only with the following service providers, each bound to GDPR-equivalent protections:

8. International Data Transfers

MailMover's infrastructure is hosted in the European Union. Payment data handled by Stripe may be transferred outside the EEA. Where required, such transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, or rely on adequacy decisions where applicable.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary:

  • Mailbox passwords and access tokens: written to a private temporary file for the duration of a run and deleted when it ends.
  • Mailbox content: never retained by us; it is streamed between providers and not written to disk by MailMover.
  • Run logs and job status: held in memory for a maximum of 24 hours, then discarded. They are also discarded if the service restarts.
  • Server and security logs: kept for a short period for security and abuse prevention.
  • Account and plan data: retained while you have a plan. Deleted on request or within 30 days of closure.
  • Billing records: retained as required by applicable tax and accounting law (typically 7 years), held by Stripe.
  • Support communications: limited retention for dispute resolution, typically 12 months.

10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures including:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit between your browser and MailMover
  • Encrypted connections to mail providers where they support it
  • Single-use, owner-only credential files deleted after each run
  • Isolated transfer processes per job
  • Access controls limiting data access to necessary personnel

No system is completely secure. We take reasonable steps to protect personal data and will notify affected users of any breach in accordance with applicable law. See also our Security page.

11. Your Rights (GDPR)

Where applicable, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time without penalty

Submit requests to legal@mailmover.eu. We respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in the EU: your national DPA).

12. Children's Privacy

MailMover is not intended for use by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we identify that data from a minor has been collected, it will be deleted promptly.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published at this URL. Material changes will be communicated by email to registered or paying users at least 14 days before taking effect.
