Security

We built MailMover on one principle: your mail and your credentials belong to you. Here is exactly how we enforce that.

Six commitments

We never store your mail

Message content streams from your source provider to your destination provider. MailMover does not write it to disk and does not read or analyse it.

Passwords live for one run

Mailbox passwords are written to a private, owner-only file for the duration of a transfer and deleted when it ends. They are never added to a database or kept for later.

Short-lived logs

Run logs and job status are held in memory for a maximum of 24 hours, then discarded. Nothing is kept permanently.

No access to your accounts

We have no standing access to your mailboxes. A transfer only connects with the details you enter for that run.

Isolated jobs

Each transfer runs as its own process with its own working directory, separate from every other user's job.

No AI training

We do not use your mailbox content, credentials or usage to train, fine-tune or improve any AI model.

How we implement it

Transport encryption

All connections to mailmover.eu are encrypted with TLS, and HTTPS is enforced. Connections to mail providers use SSL/TLS where the provider supports it.

Credential handling

Passwords are passed to the transfer process through a single-use file with owner-only permissions, not on a command line, and removed when the run ends or is stopped.

Job isolation

Each run gets its own temporary directory and process. Completed jobs are removed from memory after 24 hours.

Concurrency limits

The number of simultaneous transfers is capped, which protects the service and limits the impact of abuse.

Cookies

MailMover sets no cookies. Layout choices and saved presets (never passwords) stay in your own browser storage.

Payment security

All billing is handled directly by Stripe. We never see, store, or process your full card number or banking details. Our systems receive only a Stripe customer ID and subscription status.

Infrastructure

MailMover runs on dedicated servers hosted in the European Union, deployed and managed through a container platform.
