2. Description of Services

MailMover is a web service that copies email from one mailbox to another, for example when you change email provider. It provides:

  • Server-side transfer of mail, folders and flags between two IMAP mailboxes that you supply
  • A live progress view with pause, stop and a downloadable run log
  • Connection testing before a transfer starts
  • Free and paid access tiers, as described on the site from time to time

Transfers run on MailMover's servers. Message content passes between your two mail providers through our infrastructure and is not stored or read by us.
